30-minute meal kits from Chembur

Published 28 Jul 2026 · SABXI Studio, Chembur, Mumbai

If you live near Chembur, SABXI can put a fresh dinner on your stove before the pressure cooker whistles. Here are three meal kits built from cut-to-order produce and Mixed Packs.

Why Chembur is built for 30-minute cooking

Our first SABXI Studio in Chembur prepares every order live — cut veggies, Mixed Packs, juices, and fruit bowls — then riders deliver within a 30-minute radius. No dark-store leftovers. Mandi-fresh every morning.

Kit 1: 20-minute weekday sabzi

  • Cut pack: diced tomatoes + sliced onions + chopped palak
  • Mixed Pack: boiled potato or chana (optional)
  • Whole: green chilli / ginger if you like heat

Method: Heat oil, sauté onion, add tomato and spice, fold in palak. Serve with roti or rice. Juice on the side while it cooks.

Kit 2: Mixed Pack protein bowl

  • Mixed Pack: rajma or moong / chickpea mix
  • Cut Veg: salad cut or stir-fry mix
  • Smoothie or fruit bowl for dessert

Method: Warm the Mixed Pack with tadka, plate over rice or with salad cuts. Under 25 minutes door-to-table.

Kit 3: Breakfast / tiffin in a flash

  • Cut Fruit bowl
  • Cold-pressed juice
  • Optional: poha-friendly Mixed Pack or sandwich veg cuts

Ideal when school or WFH starts early in Chembur, Kurla, or nearby.

Local tip: Enter your Chembur / Mumbai address in the SABXI app to confirm you are inside the delivery radius. Orders run 7 AM – 9 PM.

How this beats ordering “meal kits” elsewhere

Most kits are frozen or packed days ahead. SABXI meal components are cut after you tap Order at the Chembur studio — so “30 minutes” means fresh, not just fast.
