Launch checklist: Maps & Search

Step-by-step for Google Business Profile (Chembur studio) and Search Console sitemap submission.

Internal checklist · noindex · for founders / ops

Google Business Profile and Search Console must be completed while signed into your Google account. The site is already prepared (sitemap, LocalBusiness schema, Maps link).

A. Google Business Profile (SABXI Studio Chembur)

  1. Go to business.google.com and sign in with the company Google account.
  2. Create or claim a profile. Business name: SABXI Studio (or SABXI). Category: e.g. Grocery delivery service / Fruit and vegetable store (pick the closest fit).
  3. Set the location to your Chembur studio. Use the same place as this Maps share link so reviews and Maps stay consistent.
  4. Hours: align with ops (site FAQ says roughly 7 AM – 9 PM). Phone + website: https://sabxi.com/ · email info@sabxi.com.
  5. Add 10+ photos: storefront, cutting board live prep, sealed packs, rider handoff, Mixed Packs, juices. Update weekly.
  6. Complete verification (postcard / video / phone — whatever Google offers). Then turn on messaging and ask happy customers for reviews.
  7. Optional: after the profile is public, send us the GBP URL to add under sameAs in site JSON-LD.

B. Google Search Console

  1. Open Google Search Console → Add property → URL prefix https://sabxi.com (or Domain property if you prefer DNS).
  2. Verify ownership (HTML tag, DNS TXT, or GitHub Pages HTML file). If you choose HTML meta tag, paste it to us and we will add it to index.html.
  3. After verification: Sitemaps → submit https://sabxi.com/sitemap.xml (already linked from robots.txt).
  4. Request indexing for homepage, /company/, /about/, /areas/, and top blog posts.
  5. Monitor Coverage / Experience; fix any soft 404s after Pages deploys.

C. Growth extras (need your IDs)

  • WhatsApp Business number → sticky “Chat” button
  • Instagram / LinkedIn URLs → footer + schema sameAs
  • GA4 Measurement ID → analytics on all pages
  • Google Play live: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.sabxi.sabxi
  • FSSAI licence number on footer + company page

Email those details to the site maintainer or paste in chat when ready.
