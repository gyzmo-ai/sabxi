AI-Native Platform

Intelligence at Every Layer

SABXI isn't a shop with an app bolted on — it's an AI-powered fresh commerce platform. From mandi procurement to your doorstep, every decision is optimized by machine learning.

📦

AI Supply Chain Engine

Our models analyze mandi prices, weather patterns, and festival calendars across 50+ markets in real time — sourcing the best produce at the lowest cost, automatically.

↓ 22% procurement cost
📊

Demand Forecasting

ML predicts what customers will order tomorrow — down to the gram. We stock exactly what sells, cutting spoilage and ensuring shelves are never empty.

↓ 35% food waste
💬

AI Customer Support

24/7 conversational AI handles order changes, refunds, and cut preferences in Hindi and English. Human agents only for edge cases — 94% resolution without escalation.
