Smart Spatial Design

Our layout follows a circular workflow: produce enters through storage racks, moves to the central cutting island, and finishes at the juice bar — minimizing cross-contamination and maximizing speed.

  • Storage Racks
    Slanted wooden crates display vegetables & fruits along both walls

  • Juice Center
    Professional blenders, fresh fruit display & menu boards
  • Glass Front
    Full visibility from street to counter — trust through transparency

    Market-Fresh Daily
