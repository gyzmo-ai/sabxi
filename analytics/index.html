Demand & pricing levers

Products & packaging

One average order/basket value (set in the levers) is split across product types by share % of value \u2014 e.g. Raw at 40% of a \u20b9263 basket = \u20b9105/order. Each type has its own COGS % and packaging cost. Gross profit = revenue − COGS − packaging (before shared discount, wastage, staff & overheads).

Resources & capacity

In capacity mode, headcount auto-scales with orders/day: one head per N orders/day (rounded up). Use fixed mode for roles that don't scale (e.g. manager, mandi runs).

Month-on-month projection

Set each month's rates (orders/day, basket value, COGS %, discount, delivery, wastage). The per-month COGS % is applied to that month's revenue (overriding the product-mix blend), so you can model COGS improving as you scale. Product mix, packaging, staffing capacity and overheads are shared from above and re-applied per month. Copy the last month to project the next at the same intensity, then tweak.
